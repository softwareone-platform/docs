# Large Government Agencies

You can buy Adobe products for a large government agency with the **Adobe VIP Marketplace for Large Government Agencies** product. This helps state and federal agencies in the United States and Canada buy Adobe licenses at large-agency pricing.

### Eligibility

To use this product, your organization must:

* Be a state or federal government entity.
* Be in the United States or Canada. The product is only listed in these countries.
* Order at least 100 licenses.

Other government organizations use the **Adobe VIP Marketplace for Government** product. If you order with the wrong product, the order is refused with a message telling you which product to use.

### Order details

When you order, you provide these details for your agency:

* **Agency type** – Select **Federal** or **State**. This is required.
* **Government agency name** – The name of the agency.
* **Government agency address** – The address of the agency.

You also accept the **Adobe Large Government Agencies Program Agreement - Exhibit A**, in addition to the Adobe VIP Marketplace terms.

### What you can do

With this product, you can:

* Create a new Large Government Agency account.
* Migrate a Large Government Agency account from Adobe VIP to VIP Marketplace.
* Transfer your Large Government Agency account from another reseller to SoftwareOne. For more details, see [Transfer your Adobe account to SoftwareOne](transfer-your-adobe-account-to-softwareone.md).

After your account is set up, you manage your subscriptions in the same way as other Adobe VIP Marketplace subscriptions.
