# Migrate from Adobe VIP

You can move your Adobe VIP account to Adobe VIP Marketplace by placing a migration order in the SoftwareOne Marketplace. This helps you manage your existing Adobe subscriptions, buy new licenses, and renew automatically in the SoftwareOne Marketplace.

Your company details and your active Adobe subscriptions are taken from your VIP account, so you don't need to enter them again.

### Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have the following:

* An Adobe VIP account. Accounts under other Adobe licensing programs, such as CLP, TLP, or ETLA, cannot be migrated.
* Your **Adobe VIP membership number**. You can find it in the Adobe Admin Console.
* An [active licensee](../../../modules-and-features/settings/licensees/licensee-states.md) or permission to [create a new licensee](../../../modules-and-features/settings/licensees/create-licensees.md).

### Migrate your Adobe VIP account

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Start the order**

To start the order:

1. Go to **Catalog** > **Products**.
2. Select the Adobe product, for example, **Adobe VIP Marketplace for Commercial**.
3. Select **Buy now**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Set up the migration and place the order**

In the guided purchase flow, complete the following steps:

1. **Select agreement** – Select **Create agreement**.
2. **Select licensee** – Select a licensee, then select **Next**. You can also [create a new licensee](../../../modules-and-features/settings/licensees/create-licensees.md).
3. **Adobe account setup** – Choose **Migrate Adobe VIP account**, then select **Next**.
4. **Adobe VIP membership number** – Enter your membership number, then select **Next**.
5. **Select items** – Review your active subscriptions, which are added automatically. Don't add or remove items, or change quantities. Then select **Next**.
6. **Details** – Provide reference details for the order, then select **Next**.
7. **Review order** – Review the order details, go through the terms and conditions, then select **Place order**.
8. **Summary** – Review your order summary, then select **View details** to open the order details page, or select **Close**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### If SoftwareOne prepared a draft order for you

If your SoftwareOne representative created a draft migration order for you, complete it to accept the terms of VIP Marketplace and activate your agreement.

To complete your draft order:

1. Do one of the following:
   * Select the link in the email from SoftwareOne. If you are signed in, the details page of your draft order opens.
   * Go to the [Orders](../../../modules-and-features/marketplace/orders/) page and select the Adobe order in **Draft** status.
2. Select the **Items** tab, then select **Edit** to start the purchase wizard.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1043).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Items tab of your draft order</p></figcaption></figure>

3. **Select items** – Review the items, quantities, and prices, then select **Next**. You cannot add items or change quantities in a draft order.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1039).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Select Items section</p></figcaption></figure>

4. **Order details** – Update the agreement name and other details as needed, then select **Next**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1040).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Order details section</p></figcaption></figure>

5. **Review order** – Read the terms, then select **Place order**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (1041).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Review order section</p></figcaption></figure>

### Next steps

Your migration order is submitted for processing. When it completes, your VIP Marketplace agreement is active, and you can manage your subscriptions in the SoftwareOne Marketplace.

* Your anniversary date stays the same as it was under VIP.
* Auto-renewal is turned on for all your migrated subscriptions. Check your subscriptions, and turn auto-renewal off for any that you don't want to renew. For more details, see [Change Adobe subscription auto-renewal](../manage-subscriptions/change-adobe-subscription-auto-renewal.md).
* Orders you placed in Adobe VIP can no longer be returned after you migrate.
* To add licenses or products, place a change order after the migration completes. For more details, see [Add products to your Adobe agreement](../manage-subscriptions/add-products-to-your-adobe-agreement.md).

If the order shows a message about your membership number, see [Common error messages](../common-error-messages.md).
