# Manage subscriptions

You can change your Adobe subscriptions at any time during your term: add products, change license quantities, upgrade, and turn auto-renewal on or off. This helps you keep your licenses in line with what your organization needs.

Note that you cannot place change or configuration orders on the day before your anniversary date or on the anniversary date itself. For more details, see [Key dates and deadlines](../key-dates-and-deadlines.md).
