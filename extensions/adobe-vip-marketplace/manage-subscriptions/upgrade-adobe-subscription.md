# Upgrade Adobe subscription

You can upgrade an active Adobe subscription to a higher product partway through your term by placing an upgrade order. This helps you move to the Adobe products you need without waiting for your renewal.

When you upgrade:

* You choose from the products Adobe allows your subscription to upgrade to. Adobe's suggestions are marked **Recommended**.
* You can upgrade all of your licenses or, where Adobe allows it, only some of them.
* Your anniversary date stays the same, with no new term to commit to.
* If you upgrade all of your licenses, the original subscription is terminated.
* If the upgrade creates a new subscription, auto-renewal is turned on for it.

Prices shown in the wizard are estimates. The final amount appears on your next bill.

### Prerequisites

* You are signed in with a client account. The upgrade wizard is available to client accounts only.
* The subscription and the agreement are both **Active**.
* No renewal is in place for the agreement. You cannot upgrade while a renewal is planned for your anniversary date, or for 30 days after an early renewal.

### Upgrade an Adobe subscription

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Open the subscription**

To open the subscription:

1. Go to **Marketplace** > **Subscriptions**.
2. Select the subscription you want to upgrade.
3. On the subscription details page, select the dropdown arrow, then choose **Upgrade**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Choose the upgrade and place the order**

In the guided **Upgrade subscription** flow, complete the following steps:

1. **Upgrade from** – Check the subscription you are upgrading, then select **Next**.
2. **Upgrade to** – Select the product to upgrade to and enter the **New Quantity**, then select **Next**. Where Adobe doesn't allow a partial upgrade, the quantity must match the quantity you hold.
3. **Promotions** – Choose a discount code for the upgraded item, or enter your own. The code that gives you the lowest price is pre-selected, and the discounted price is shown. Select **Next**.
4. **Split billing** – If your subscription has split billing, choose how to allocate billing for this order, then select **Next**. This step only appears for subscriptions with split billing.
5. **Details** – (Optional) Enter an **Additional ID** and **Notes** to help you identify the order, then select **Next**.
6. **Review order** – Review the effect on both subscriptions and the estimated prices, then select **Place order**.
7. **Summary** – Select **View order** to open the order details page.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Next steps

Your upgrade order is submitted for processing. The subscription and the agreement change from **Active** to **Updating**, and you cannot place further orders until the order completes.

When the order completes, the upgraded licenses are on the new product, and your anniversary date is unchanged. You can follow the order on its details page.

Note that an upgrade cannot be returned like a new or renewal order. For more details, see [Returns and termination](../returns-and-termination/).
