# Global customers

You can buy Adobe licenses for users in more than one country under one Adobe account by setting the account up as a global customer. This helps organizations with users in several countries manage their Adobe licenses together.

### How global customer accounts are set up

A global customer account can be set up in one of two ways. The following table describes them:

| Setup                                | How it works                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **One agreement**                    | All licenses are bought under the main account, on one agreement, whichever country your users are in. This is the simpler setup, and the one most new global customers use.                     |
| **An agreement for each deployment** | The account has a main account and one or more deployments. A deployment is a location, such as a country, with its own currency. The main account and each deployment have their own agreement. |

Deployments come from an earlier Adobe requirement to state which country each license was used in. Adobe no longer requires this, so you don't need deployments to buy licenses for users in different countries.

### Accounts with deployments

If your account has deployments:

* You can place orders against the main account or any deployment.
* Each deployment uses a local price list for its region and currency, so you only see the products available in that region.
* Billing follows each deployment's location and currency.
* Returns are limited to the deployment the order was placed for.

You can move to one agreement at your anniversary date. To do this, turn off auto-renewal for all subscriptions in your deployment agreements, and buy the same products again under the main account. Contact SoftwareOne to plan the move.

### Set up a global customer account

To set up your Adobe account as a global customer, contact SoftwareOne.

You can check whether your account is a global customer on the **Adobe** tab of your agreement, under **Global customer**. If your account has deployments, each agreement's **Parameters** tab also shows its deployment ID and all the deployments of your account. For more details, see [Agreement and subscription details](agreement-and-subscription-details.md).
