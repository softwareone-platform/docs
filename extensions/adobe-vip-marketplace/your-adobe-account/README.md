# Your Adobe account

You can see details of your Adobe account that come directly from Adobe on the **Adobe** tab of your agreement. This helps you check your 3-year commitment, linked membership, global customer setting, and discount codes in one place.

To open the Adobe tab:

1. Go to **Marketplace** > **Agreements**.
2. Select the Adobe agreement.
3. Select the **Adobe** tab.

Under **Manage account**, the tab has four sections: **3-year commitment**, **Linked membership**, **Global customer**, and **Discounts**.

### 3-year commitment

This section shows your 3-year commitment as Adobe currently records it. The following table describes the three groups of details:

| Group                    | What it shows                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Current commitment**   | Your active 3-year commitment: its status, minimum licenses and consumables, and start and end dates.                           |
| **Commitment request**   | A commitment you have been invited to, or a request to increase your commitment: its status, minimums, and start and end dates. |
| **Recommitment request** | An invitation to commit for another 3 years when your current commitment ends: its status and minimums.                         |

The following table describes the statuses you might see:

| Status           | Meaning                                                                          |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Requested**    | An invitation has been sent. Accept it in the Adobe Admin Console.               |
| **Accepted**     | You accepted the invitation, but you haven't reached the minimum quantities yet. |
| **Committed**    | Your 3-year commitment is active, and its discount level applies.                |
| **Declined**     | The invitation was declined.                                                     |
| **Noncompliant** | You are below your committed minimum.                                            |
| **Expired**      | The commitment or invitation has ended.                                          |

To request a 3-year commitment, increase it, or recommit, contact SoftwareOne. For more details, see [How do I accept my 3-year commitment invitation?](../faqs/how-do-i-accept-my-3-year-commitment-invitation.md).

### Linked membership

This section shows whether your Adobe account is part of a linked membership, which lets several Adobe accounts combine their quantities for volume discounts. It shows the membership **Type** (**Standard** or **Consortium**), this account's role in the membership, and the creation date.

To create a linked membership, contact SoftwareOne. Note that a linked membership cannot be created for an account with a 3-year commitment, and it cannot be changed once it is created.

### Global customer

This section shows whether your Adobe account is set up as a global customer, so that you can buy licenses for users in more than one country. For more details, see [Global customers](global-customers.md).

To set up your account as a global customer, contact SoftwareOne.

### Discounts

This section lists the flexible discount codes available to you. For more details, see [Adobe discount codes](adobe-discount-codes.md).
