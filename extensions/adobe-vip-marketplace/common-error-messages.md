# Common error messages

You can find the error messages you might see when you reduce, return, or terminate Adobe licenses, and how to resolve them. This helps you fix the problem and place your order again.

***

**Error message** – "Cannot return the entire quantity of all subscriptions in this order. Consider disabling auto-renewal for these subscriptions instead using a Configuration Order."

**Cause** – You placed a termination order for a subscription (or an agreement) where at least one license is outside the 14-day return window, so not every license can be returned.

**Resolution** – To stop the subscription at your anniversary date, turn off auto-renewal with a configuration order. For more details, see [Disable Adobe auto-renewal](tutorials-and-videos/disable-adobe-auto-renewal.md). To return the licenses that are still returnable, reduce the subscription's quantity instead. For more details, see [Reduce Adobe license quantity](tutorials-and-videos/downsize-adobe-subscription.md).

***

**Error message** – "Adobe rejected the return because it would reduce the three-year commitment below its minimum commit quantity: …"

**Cause** – You have a 3-year commitment, and the return would take your licenses below your committed minimum. Nothing was returned.

**Resolution** – Reduce by a smaller quantity, so you stay at or above your minimum. If you need to go below it, contact SoftwareOne.

***

**Error message** – "The downsize could not be completed after part of it was already returned to Adobe. Returned lines were not undone. …"

**Cause** – Adobe refused part of a reduction after other parts had already been returned. The message lists each product as `RETURNED`, `PARTIAL`, `NOT PROCESSED`, or `DEFERRED`.

**Resolution** – The returns already made stay in place and are credited. Check the subscription's **Current quantity** and **Renewal quantity**, then place a new change order for anything that still needs changing. Contact SoftwareOne if you need help.

For more details, see [Downsize, termination, and return policies](downsize-and-termination-policies.md).
