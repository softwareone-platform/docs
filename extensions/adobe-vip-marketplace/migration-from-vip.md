If you are currently ordering subscriptions through the Adobe Value Incentive Plan (VIP) buying program, we recommend you familiarize yourself with the differences between the Adobe VIP and VIP Marketplace programs.

### Migrate your account

You can migrate your Adobe VIP account to VIP Marketplace in one of two ways:

* **With your SoftwareOne representative** – Your representative prepares a draft order for you. To complete it, see [How do I activate my VIP Marketplace agreement?](faqs/how-do-i-activate-my-vip-marketplace-agreement.md).
* **Yourself** – Place a new order in the SoftwareOne Marketplace. Go to **Catalog** > **Products**, select the Adobe product, and select **Buy now**. Create an agreement, and on the **Adobe account setup** step, choose **Migrate Adobe VIP account**. Then enter your **Adobe VIP membership number** and complete the order.

The migration order includes your existing VIP subscriptions. Do not add or remove items or change quantities in the migration order. After it completes, make changes with a change order.

Only Adobe VIP accounts can be migrated. Accounts under other Adobe licensing programs, such as CLP, TLP, or ETLA, cannot be migrated.
