# Renew Adobe subscriptions now

You can renew your Adobe subscriptions before your anniversary date in the renewal wizard. This helps you lock in today's prices, so you avoid any price increase before your anniversary date.

When you renew now:

* The renewal takes effect today. It is invoiced in the next weekly billing cycle.
* Your anniversary date moves forward a year.
* Any further renewal orders on the agreement also renew now.
* Adding products or increasing quantities is handled in separate orders after the renewal.

Because each of these orders is priced on its own, your licenses may not reach the same volume discount level as they would if they were renewed together. When you renew at your anniversary date, everything you renew, including any quantity changes or new products, is priced together for the best available volume discount. For more details, see [Renew Adobe subscriptions at your anniversary date](renew-adobe-subscriptions-at-your-anniversary-date.md).

### Prerequisites

* You are signed in with a client account. The renewal wizard is available to client accounts only.
* The agreement is **Active**. While another order is processing, the agreement is **Updating**, and you cannot start a renewal.
* It is between 30 and 2 days before your anniversary date (US Pacific time). For more details, see [Key dates and deadlines](../key-dates-and-deadlines.md).

### Renew your subscriptions now

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Open the renewal wizard**

To open the renewal wizard:

1. Go to **Marketplace** > **Agreements**.
2. Select the Adobe agreement you want to renew.
3. On the agreement details page, select the dropdown arrow, then choose **Renew**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Renew and place the order**

In the guided **Renew** flow, complete the following steps:

1. **Timing** – Select **Renew now**, then select **Next**. This choice applies to the whole renewal and cannot be changed after you place your first order.
2. **Renewal** – For each subscription, turn **Renew** on or off, then select **Next**.
3. **Items** – Under **Renewal qty**, set the quantities you want to renew, then select **Next**. You cannot renew more than you hold now.
4. **Promotions** – For each item, choose a discount code, or select **No code**. Codes you already hold are marked **Inherited**. Select **Next**.
5. **Details** – (Optional) Enter an **Additional ID** and **Notes** to help you identify the order, then select **Next**.
6. **Review order** – Review the items and estimated prices, then select **Place order**.
7. **Summary** – Select **View order** to open the order details page.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Add licenses or products after you renew

Adobe handles a renewal and any additions as separate orders, so one order cannot both renew and add. To add licenses or new products after you renew now:

1. Open the renewal wizard again. The **Timing** step shows **Renew now (confirmed)**.
2. On the **Items** step, add the products or increase the quantities you need.
3. Complete the remaining steps and select **Place order**.

You can also use a follow-up order to renew licenses you didn't renew the first time. The **Items** step shows how many licenses of each subscription are already renewed. You can place follow-up renewal orders until 2 days before your original anniversary date.

Note that each follow-up order is priced separately, so it may not receive the same discount as a single combined renewal.

### Next steps

Your renewal takes effect today, and your anniversary date moves forward a year. The renewal is invoiced in the next weekly billing cycle. You can follow the order on its details page.

After you renew now:

* You cannot renew fewer licenses than you already renewed early.
* Change, configuration, and termination orders, and upgrades, are locked until your original anniversary date (the anniversary date you had before you renewed now).
* If you have a 3-year commitment, your renewal must keep you at or above your committed minimum.
