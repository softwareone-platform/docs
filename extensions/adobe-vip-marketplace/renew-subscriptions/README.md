# Renew subscriptions

You can renew your Adobe subscriptions at your anniversary date, renew them early, or renew them late if they expired. This helps you keep your licenses without interruption and choose when you're billed.

If auto-renewal is on, your subscriptions renew automatically on your anniversary date. Use the renewal wizard to change what renews, add products, or apply discount codes. For the renewal window and other dates, see [Key dates and deadlines](../key-dates-and-deadlines.md).
