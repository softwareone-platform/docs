# Renew Adobe subscriptions at your anniversary date

You can plan your Adobe renewal in the renewal wizard so that it takes effect on your anniversary date. This helps you choose which subscriptions renew, change renewal quantities, add products, and apply discount codes, with nothing billed until your anniversary date.

Everything you renew is priced together for the best available volume discount, including any quantity changes or new products you add. Pricing reflects the rates in effect on your anniversary date.

To renew straight away instead, see [Renew Adobe subscriptions now](renew-adobe-subscriptions-now.md).

### Prerequisites

* You are signed in with a client account. The renewal wizard is available to client accounts only.
* The agreement is **Active**. While another order is processing, the agreement is **Updating**, and you cannot start a renewal.
* It is between 30 and 2 days before your anniversary date (US Pacific time). For more details, see [Key dates and deadlines](../key-dates-and-deadlines.md).

### Plan your renewal for your anniversary date

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Open the renewal wizard**

To open the renewal wizard:

1. Go to **Marketplace** > **Agreements**.
2. Select the Adobe agreement you want to renew.
3. On the agreement details page, select the dropdown arrow, then choose **Renew**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Plan the renewal and place the order**

In the guided **Renew** flow, complete the following steps:

1. **Timing** – Select **Renew at your anniversary date (recommended)**, then select **Next**. This choice applies to the whole renewal and cannot be changed after you place your first order.
2. **Renewal** – For each subscription, turn **Renew** on or off, then select **Next**. A subscription with **Renew** turned off stops renewing and expires on your anniversary date.
3. **Items** – Under **Renewal qty**, change the quantities you want to renew. To add a product, select **Add items**, choose the items in **Select items**, then select **Next**. Adobe's suggestions are marked **Recommended**.
4. **Promotions** – For each item, choose a discount code, or select **No code**. Codes you already hold are marked **Inherited**. Select **Next**.
5. **Details** – (Optional) Enter an **Additional ID** and **Notes** to help you identify the order, then select **Next**.
6. **Review order** – Review the items and estimated prices, then select **Place order**.
7. **Summary** – Select **View order** to open the order details page.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Next steps

Your renewal is set up, and nothing is billed until your anniversary date. On your anniversary date, Adobe renews your subscriptions as planned.

* **To change the plan**, open the renewal wizard again and place another order. The **Timing** step shows **Renew at your anniversary date (confirmed)**, and your changes join the same renewal. You can change the plan until 2 days before your anniversary date.
* **Products you add** start on your anniversary date. Until then, their current quantity is 0.
* **While your renewal is set up**, you cannot place change, configuration, or termination orders, or upgrade a subscription. Make renewal changes through the renewal wizard instead.

Note that some products cannot renew at the anniversary date, such as Stock Credit Packs. The wizard leaves them out of your plan. If you have a 3-year commitment, your plan must keep you at or above your committed minimum.
